# Three-way audit, 2026-08-20 — what stands and what is retracted

Three independent audits ran against the ambient-noise work: geophysics
(`AUDIT_GEOPHYSICS.md`), numerics (`AUDIT_NUMERICS.md`), claims-vs-evidence
(`AUDIT_CLAIMS.md`). Each was given the errors already caught that day so it knew
the *class* of mistake to hunt, and each was told a refutation was worth more than
a clean report. This file reconciles them. **Where it disagrees with a script
docstring, a figure caption, or an older note, this file wins.**

`AMBIENT_FIG7C_STATUS.md` is untouched and stands: every number in it verified
exactly (peak 6.1314 at 5850 m/s, 2.7523/2.8314 at 3200, null95 6.3241,
p = 0.1470 over 10,000 permutations), including §4.

---

## 1. Retracted

**1.1 The Deep arrival is not at 1675 m/s.** Measured directly — envelope-peak
pick per trace, lag regressed on separation, 100–450 m:

| gather | v (m/s) | r |
|---|---|---|
| `deep_record_section.npz` (dense, 172 traces) | **1443** | +0.979 |
| `deep_record_section_pub.npz` | 1532 | +0.960 |
| `deep_section_depth_pair200out.npz` (TVD, outbound) | 1509 | +0.916 |
| `deep_section_depth_pair200ret.npz` (TVD, **return limb**) | 1485 | +0.902 |

Four gathers, both fibre limbs: **1443–1537 m/s**, 9–16 % below the reported
figure. `1675` is hard-coded as `V_MARK` / `V_ARR` / `--v-ref` in five scripts, so
every figure drew a wrong line.

**1.2 The cause is a lag-window truncation, and it is bigger than one number.**
`MAX_LAG_SECONDS = 0.35` cannot hold a 700 m offset below 1934 m/s. On a
300–6000 m/s grid that puts **71 of 229 trial velocities** — everything below
2075 m/s — in a regime where the far offsets' gates fall outside the window.
`nanmedian` then ran over whichever offsets survived, and the survivors are the
NEAR offsets, which sit closest to the zero-lag lobe and score high. The score
therefore rises smoothly as velocity falls **for a purely geometric reason**.

That is the shape of the "pedestal" this project has fought throughout, and the
Deep arrival sits entirely inside the affected range. `moveout_scores` now refuses
to score a velocity unless every offset's gate lies wholly inside the window, and
`--max-lag` widens it. All Deep aggregates are being re-measured at ±2.5 s, which
holds 700 m down to 280 m/s. **Until those land, no low-velocity number from this
scan should be quoted.**

In `deep_cc_steps.py` and `nano_long_stack.py` the same situation was worse:
`argmin(|lags - centre|)` silently **clamps to the window edge** instead of
failing, so out-of-range velocities were scored on the edge of the correlation.

**1.3 causal/acausal 2.34 is not evidence.** For src 400 it is 2.338 at the peak
but **2.138 at 3200 m/s where p = 0.198**, grid-wide median **2.007**, and largest
at **825 m/s (3.321)**. It is a property of the gather, not of the arrival. No
script produces it; `REPRODUCE.md:72` credits `deep_pervel.py`, which computes no
such thing.

**1.4 Every Nano ambient result is void — the virtual source was in the air.**
`nano_find_wellhead.py` puts fibre entry at channel **73**; `nano_long_stack.npz`,
`deep_record_section_nano{,cm}.npz`, `fk_downgoing_nano` and `nano_ambient_cc`'s
default all used channel **10**. This kills the **p = 0.0474** positive *and* the
25 h **N^+0.023** null that superseded it. An air source manufactures a null, so
the null was not evidence either. **The cemented fibre has never been tested from
a downhole source.** Guards now refuse an air source on both fibres.

**1.5 The f-k down/up ratio is not an energy ratio and its ">1" is selected.**
Forcing both quadrants returns exact reciprocals (0.608/1.644, 0.639/1.564,
0.507/1.972) and the script kept the larger. Calibrated against known input, a
**1:1 balanced synthetic reports 6.06, not 1.0**, and the statistic is
non-monotonic (4:1 scores 6.52, above pure-downgoing 5.90). The *quadrant label*
is independently correct (downgoing = k·f < 0, verified 29:1 and 84:1 on
synthetics, and all four production runs picked the right one) — the magnitude is
not interpretable. A plain quadrant energy ratio now accompanies it: **2.3–2.5
Deep, 1.03 Nano**.

**1.6 `r = 0.948` does not "reproduce Lellouch's Figure 9."** It is
corrcoef(depth, velocity) on picks — a depth–velocity trend against an undigitised
curve. The `.npz` key `r` is a different quantity, **−0.4453**. It survived in five
places including the negative-result abstract.

**1.7 "1547 m/s, independently measured" is not independent.**
`deep_dvv_frozen_trajectory.json` shows the semblance search was confined to
**1300–1800 m/s** on tube-wave grounds. It could not have returned anything else.
1675 vs 1547 also differ by less than the 138 m/s scatter across source channels.

**1.8 Four numbers no product supports**, plus the gauge-response formula
(`sinc(L/λ)`, not `sinc(π k L)` — off by π, though the 0.1–1.1 % differential it
was used for is right).

## 2. What survives, and is stronger for having been attacked

- **The Deep arrival is real.** p < 1/4001 on an independent seed; **59 of 229**
  velocities clear their own per-velocity null against 11.5 expected; it is not
  the pedestal (0.96 vs 3.33); it appears on **both fibre limbs at the same TVD**
  with r ≈ 0.90; robust to gate width 4–50 ms. Its *velocity* is retracted, not
  its existence.
- **The published R±10 neighbour sum hurts on Deep**, with the mechanism measured:
  the sum passes k = 0 at 1.000 but the arrival at **0.622 at 20 Hz** (first null
  39 Hz, inside the band); low-k energy share rises **20.2 % → 36.4 %**; usable
  range falls 506 m → 298 m.
- **Geometry.** cos(inclination) integration reproduces the spreadsheet TVD column
  to **0.0002 %**; the 5° near-vertical cutoff admits ≤ **0.38 %** velocity bias.
  `safod_geometry` passed 13 of 13 checks.
- **No spatial aliasing anywhere** — worst case 25 samples per wavelength.
- **Lag sign and f-k quadrant are correct**, verified by synthetic round trip.

## 3. Open, and the most important item on this page

**The detectability test has never been run on the Deep arrival.** It is the test
that killed every negative result here — a coherent arrival must grow as N^+0.50.
The Deep arrival's raw-score exponent is **N^+0.128**, *below* the 2024–25
baseline's +0.158 that this project calls "FLAT". That is not a refutation, because
the raw score is not the detectability statistic, but it means the one test that
would settle the Deep result has not been applied to it. Everything else in §2 is
consistent with a real arrival that does not accumulate — which would need
explaining.

Also open:

- **The archive scan null is conservative and under-powered.** 11/240 vs 12.0
  expected is right (two-sided p = 0.917), but the 240 p-values are non-uniform,
  **KS p = 0.001**, shifted high. No power curve was ever computed.
- **N^+0.042 was fitted at 5950 m/s — the grid edge**, a statistic this project
  itself calls void. Detectability at 3200 m/s was never measured.
- **Tube-wave identity is UNCHECKABLE here.** No borehole diameter, fluid, casing
  or cement data exist in the workspace. ~1500 m/s is *consistent with* a
  fluid-guided mode; it is not established as one.
- **Channel spacing disagrees between sources**: spreadsheet step **2.048911 m** vs
  HDF5 `SpatialSamplingInterval` **2.0419047 m**, 0.343 %. Offsets and depths are
  currently on different metres.
- **`aggregate_deepA_src1600`'s receivers (1624–1943) cross the fibre reversal at
  1700.** It is not interpretable and must not feed the "direction flips at 1702"
  argument.

## 4. Where the audits disagree

On the R±10 neighbour sum, `AUDIT_GEOPHYSICS.md` marks it CONFIRMED with a direct
measurement of the mechanism (§2 above); `AUDIT_CLAIMS.md` §1 row 19 marks the same
claim unsupported. The geophysics finding rests on a measured transfer function,
the claims finding on the *selected* down/up ratio now retracted in §1.5, so the
measurement is the better evidence — but the disagreement is recorded rather than
resolved silently, per this repo's convention.

## 5. The band question, which came out the opposite way

5–20 Hz was suspected of being the wrong band for the central negative. It is not.
`nano_mode_identification.txt` measures 3300 m/s at 15–25 Hz and 2950 m/s at
35–80 Hz, a slowness trend of 0.483 µs m⁻¹ Hz⁻¹; extrapolated into 5–20 Hz that
gives **3102–3238 m/s**, i.e. Lellouch's 3200. **Testing 3200 m/s at 5–20 Hz on the
cemented fibre was band-appropriate**, and the negative is not an artefact of band
choice.

The corollary is a real error in the other direction: **2950 m/s is a 30–60 Hz
number and should never have been the a-priori ambient target.** The Nano rerun
uses ~3200 m/s.

The band caveat *does* stand for the Deep fibre, where 5–20 Hz and 15–30 Hz differ
and the band-matched active value is 1592 m/s.
