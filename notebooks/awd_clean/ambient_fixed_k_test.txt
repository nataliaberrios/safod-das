=== low-k centroid across bands (the discriminating measurement) ===
  5-12 Hz    band centre  8.50 Hz -> |k| centroid 0.00173 cyc/m (1.21 cells)
  12-20 Hz   band centre 16.00 Hz -> |k| centroid 0.00175 cyc/m (1.23 cells)
  frequency ratio 1.882 | wavenumber ratio 1.011
  a WAVE at fixed v predicts k_ratio = f_ratio = 1.882
  a STATIC pattern at fixed k predicts k_ratio = 1.000
  -> closer to FIXED k: a static spatial pattern. It has no
     velocity, so no velocity-domain filter can separate it BY
     velocity -- which is why F-K, tau-p, rank-k, PCC, PWS and
     flat-event removal all failed the same way.

     SENSITIVITY: the test had the range to see a wave and did
     not. A 3200 m/s arrival sits at 1.86 cells at 8.5 Hz and
     3.50 cells at 16.0 Hz, both inside the window measured, so
     a dominant wave would have moved the centroid and did not.

     LIMIT, and it matters for what comes next: this says the
     DOMINANT low-k feature is not a wave. It does NOT say no
     arrival exists -- a weak arrival can hide beneath a feature
     holding ~39 % of the in-band energy. The implication is
     about METHOD, not absence: because the contaminant is a
     fixed SPATIAL pattern, the removal has to be spatial --
     estimate and divide out the static per-channel response --
     not a velocity filter. C2_PERMEABILITY_FOLLOWUP.md already
     proposes exactly that calibration and records 5x headroom
     from it; this is independent motivation for doing it.

=== secondary diagnostic: peak-per-frequency model fit ===
  (The PRIMARY result is the low-k centroid comparison above. This
   section is a weaker diagnostic kept for the record; if it reports no
   verdict that does NOT retract the centroid result.)
  This diagnostic produced NO INFORMATIVE COMPARISON in any band.
  Every band failed the permutation control, the fit test, or both,
  so k_peak(f) is noise and neither model is supported BY THIS
  DIAGNOSTIC. The centroid measurement above is unaffected: it uses
  a different quantity in a different region of wavenumber.

  WHY, and what to do instead. Excluding +-2 cells around k = 0 to
  avoid a trivial answer pushed the per-frequency peak out to
  ~0.019 cycles/m -- about 13 cells from zero, i.e. the genuine
  surface-wave energy at 267-640 m/s -- which is not the pedestal
  this test was written to characterise. The right measurement is
  the k-marginal in the FIRST FEW non-zero cells, compared between
  bands: if the same low-k cells dominate in 5-12 Hz and 12-20 Hz,
  the feature is at fixed k. The band-shift already observed (the
  ~28 %% share moving from the 3000-4000 m/s bin to 6000-10000 m/s
  when the band was raised) is consistent with fixed k, but it is an
  observation awaiting that test, not a result.
