--- 2. white-noise floor and 3. real-data score ---
    white noise: 8 Gaussian realizations through the complete operator
    real data: 8 duration-matched 12 s excerpts from 2024-12-20

config                  white median   white 95th   real median real/white
hard (production)             0.1334       0.2196        0.0837       0.63
taper f=1 Hz, v=5%            0.1050       0.1677        0.0622       0.59

taper f=4 Hz, v=20%           0.0389       0.1213        0.0212       0.54

  'real/white' is the quantity that decides whether the fan adds information.
  A taper is only useful if it raises this ratio -- i.e. suppresses the
  manufactured floor by more than it suppresses the real score.

--- interpretation ---
  Tapering changes the manufactured white-noise score by 0.29x at best (taper f=4 Hz, v=20%).
  real/white ratio: hard 0.63 -> best taper 0.54
  The taper substantially suppresses the ringing floor.

  Either way this does not license the F-K result. A velocity fan passes
  only energy consistent with its own band, so its output resembles that
  moveout whatever the edge shape. The gate is the pre-filter channel
  scramble on real data, which the production fan fails.
