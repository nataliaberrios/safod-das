  VERDICT: the dominant spatial pattern is essentially the SAME across
  months. An earth or site noise pattern does not hold its shape over
  that span while noise conditions change; a fixed optical/electronic
  response does. The interrogator (or the fixed per-channel response
  of this installation) is the leading explanation for the static
  fixed-k contaminant, and the remedy follows directly: estimate that
  per-channel response ONCE and divide it out, which is the spatial
  calibration C2_PERMEABILITY_FOLLOWUP.md already proposes.
