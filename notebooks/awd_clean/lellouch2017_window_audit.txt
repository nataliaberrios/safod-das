  NO picked arrival falls inside the census's 2017 window -- every
  arrival is later than the 2.5 s cut, so that arm IS pre-event
  noise. Both arms of the census are therefore noise and the
  cross-epoch comparison is NOT confounded by signal presence.
  Its other stated limits still apply: only ~5 s of 2017 data, and
  gauge length 10 m vs 16.335 m (a 0.1-1.1 %% effect at 3200 m/s).
