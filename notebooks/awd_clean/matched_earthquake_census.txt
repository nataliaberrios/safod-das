=== NOT TESTABLE WITH THE RELEASED DATA ===
  Total post-arrival 2017 data across both records: 0.90 s, which is
  less than one 2.0 s analysis window. The released records place
  their arrivals at the very end (4.10 s and 4.92 s of 5.00 s), so
  there is no way to form a 2017 arm that actually contains the
  earthquake. Concatenating the full records instead would give a
  2017 arm that is ~90 %% pre-event noise compared against a 2024-25
  arm that is mostly earthquake -- the signal-presence confound this
  test exists to remove, merely inverted, and it would have produced
  a confident and meaningless number.

  THIS DOES NOT LEAVE THE QUESTION OPEN. lellouch2017_window_audit.py
  established that the ambient census's 2017 window (first 2.5 s) is
  genuinely PRE-EVENT -- both arrivals are later than the cut -- so
  both arms of that census were noise and the ambient-vs-ambient
  comparison is already the matched one. Shortening WIN_S to fit the
  0.9 s available is not a fix: at 5 Hz that is under 5 cycles, from
  a single window, which cannot support a wavenumber budget.
