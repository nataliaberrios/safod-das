  at L=10 the two epochs do not overlap: every 2017 value (min 0.524)
  exceeds every 2024-25 value (max 0.435).

  FINDING: the 2024-25 ambient wavefield decorrelates over a MUCH shorter
  distance -- median 4 channels against 26 in 2017.
  This is directly disqualifying for Figure 7c: a virtual-source gather
  spanning 50-700 m requires spatial coherence across those offsets, and
  a field that decorrelates within ~10 m cannot supply it, however well
  the instrument performs.

  It also points AWAY from B' (a noisier interrogator). Self-noise is
  incoherent at ALL separations and would depress L=1 as well; here L=1
  is HIGHER in 2024-25 (0.907 vs 0.775). The loss is in spatial structure,
  not in noise amplitude -- which is the signature of a changed SOURCE
  FIELD, i.e. explanation A.

  STRENGTH OF EVIDENCE: suggestive, not established. n = 2 for 2017 and the
  two records disagree sharply at L=1 (0.562 vs 0.988), so the 2017 side rests
  on very little. The separation at L=10 is complete but 2-vs-6 gives an
  exact one-tailed rank p of only 0.071.

  LIMITS: 2017 rests on 2 records and ~2 s of pre-event data each; the
  gauge-length confound is uncorrected; and pre-event windows of a
  triggered record are not the same population as continuous ambient.
